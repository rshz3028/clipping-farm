    How to read this

    vs median: views in the first 2 days (7 when available) divided by your typical video at that age. 2.0× = twice as good.

    % watched: average share of the video watched (loops excluded).

    Hook: share of viewers still watching after ~3 seconds. The first 2–3 s decide whether people swipe away.

    Feed: share of views from the Shorts feed. Low = YouTube isn't showing it to new people yet.

    Swipe: Studio-only "viewed vs swiped away". Shown only if you drop a Studio CSV export into work\analytics\studio\.

    Diagnosis: one problem at a time: barely shown → topic/niche; weak hook → first 2–3 s; slow middle → pacing; small topic → bigger subject; no new subs → give a reason to follow.
