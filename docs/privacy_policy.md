## About this app
[APP NAME] is a private desktop tool used only by its owner, [YOUR NAME OR CHANNEL NAME], to upload

## YouTube API Services
This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of
Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What data the app accesses
- The app requests only one permission: uploading videos to the signed-in user's YouTube channel
  (scope `youtube.upload`).
- It uploads only videos created and approved by the channel owner, together with their title and
  description.
- It does not read, collect or store any other YouTube data: no viewer data, no comments, no
  analytics, no data about other channels or users.

## How data is stored and shared
- The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file. It is never
  sent anywhere except to Google to authorise uploads.
- No personal data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- No cookies or tracking are used by this app.

## Revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page:
https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access from the computer.
